# Delivery outcomes

## Interactive 3D car viewing — Complete
- Display a realistic, high-quality 3D sports car in a full-screen 3D hero section.
- Enable full 360-degree horizontal and vertical rotation. Rotate the car using mouse drag and mobile finger swipe. Support pinch-to-zoom and mouse-wheel zoom. Add smooth rotation with natural movement.
- Include front, rear, side, top and interior camera views, reset camera and fullscreen controls.
- The car must be genuinely interactive, allowing visitors to rotate, zoom and inspect it in real time; this must be a complete, functional 360-degree 3D car website, not a static mockup or video.

## Real-time car customization and feature inspection — Complete
- Change car paint colours in real time and customize alloy wheels.
- Explore the interior, dashboard, steering wheel and seats.
- Add interactive hotspots to highlight important car features.
- Open doors, bonnet and boot if supported by the 3D model; do not show nonfunctional articulation controls if the model does not support them.

## Cinematic, scroll-driven showcase — Complete
- Create cinematic scroll-driven camera movements and rotate the car automatically while scrolling.
- Zoom into headlights, wheels, grille and interior.
- Use smooth transitions between website sections and finish with a complete 360° car showcase.

## Luxury automotive website and content — Complete
- Use a premium black and dark-grey theme, modern typography and elegant layouts, with a minimalist luxury automotive aesthetic.
- Provide professional navigation and a customization panel.
- Include car specifications and feature sections.

## Responsive performance and device support — Complete
- The website must be fully responsive on desktop and Android, with touch-friendly controls.
- Optimize 3D models and textures, provide a loading progress indicator, and deliver smooth performance with mobile optimization.
- Lazy-load the 3D experience and provide a fallback for unsupported devices.

## Production-ready implementation — Complete
- Use React or Next.js, Three.js and React Three Fiber, GSAP ScrollTrigger, Blender GLB/GLTF 3D models, and Tailwind CSS.
- All buttons and controls must work. Deliver clean, production-ready code with a premium automotive experience.
